LESS SETUP. MORE SHARING.

01 / NEARBY

Same network. No account.

Open Bolt on both PCs, make them Visible, and choose a nearby device. The recipient reviews and approves each transfer. Network isolation or multicast blocking can prevent discovery.

02 / ANYWHERE

A little further? Go online.

Sign in for internet sharing, enter the recipient’s exact email, and select an online device. Bolt attempts a direct connection and can use an encrypted relay when needed.

Google sign-in is currently in testing mode. Public access is not yet enabled. Email-code delivery is test-only.

03 / OUT OF THE WAY

Right-click. Share. Keep going.

Send files and folders from Explorer’s “Share with Bolt” menu. Close the window and Bolt stays in the system tray. On Windows 11, look under “Show more options.”

A NOTE ON EARLY ACCESS

Small app. Honest boundaries.

Bolt supports files and folders up to 10 GiB. Both devices must be online and the recipient must approve. Interrupted-transfer resume is not available yet. The shared test relay has a 500 MB monthly allowance; direct transfers do not use it. Installers are not Authenticode-signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

Requires .NET Framework 4.8 and Microsoft Visual C++ v14 x64 runtime. Nearby device names are self-reported: only accept transfers you expect.
